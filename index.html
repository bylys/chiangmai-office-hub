Monthly Overhead Bills Cadence in Thailand / กำหนดชำระบิลรายเดือน

1st: Office Rent ฿45,000

Transfer via Bangkok Bank (BBL) to Landlord Somchai. Save slip as PDF.

10th: PEA Electricity ฿14,200

Scan QR on PEA Smart Plus App or K PLUS / SCB (CA 02003884192).

15th: MWA Water ฿1,450

Scan barcode on utility slip via mobile banking app.

18th: AIS Fibre 1Gbps ฿2,640

Corporate e-Bill payment via QR PromptPay. Get tax invoice.

20th: True Backup SIM ฿1,060

True iService corporate account or Mobile Banking bill pay.

Fridays: P'Noi Cleaning ฿1,800/wk

PromptPay to 082-190-7765 after Friday 17:00 clean is completed.
